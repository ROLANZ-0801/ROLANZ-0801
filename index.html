The Padi Growth Curve

In Malaysia, self-sufficiency in rice is critical for national food security. The high-yielding variety MR297 (developed by MARDI) follows a biological logistic growth curve. By modeling height growth $f(x)$ over 120 Days After Planting (DAP), we apply the first derivative $f'(x)$ (growth rate in cm/day) to identify the inflection point. At this point, plant metabolic demand peaks, marking the absolute ideal window for critical nitrogen fertilization.

Source Reference: MARDI Manual Penanaman Padi Sawah Keupayaan Tinggi, National Agrofood Policy 2021-2030 (DAN 2.0).

Mathematical Models

1. Height Function $f(x)$ (Logistic)
$$f(x) = \frac{L}{1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)}}$$

Where $L$ is the Carrying Capacity (max height), $k$ is the growth rate constant, and $x_0$ is the midpoint (inflection day).

2. Growth Rate $f'(x)$ (First Derivative)
$$f'(x) = \frac{L \cdot k \cdot e^{-k(x - x_0)}}{\left(1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)}\right)^2} = k \cdot f(x)\left(1 - \frac{f(x)}{L}\right)$$

This derivative measures the instantaneous plant growth rate in cm/day at any day $x$.
